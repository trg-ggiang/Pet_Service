# Project Brief

## 1. Ten du an

Pet Service

## 2. Mo ta ngan

Pet Service la mot he thong quan ly dich vu thu cung, ho tro customer self-service va van hanh noi bo cho phong kham/don vi dich vu.

He thong hien co dang di theo huong quan ly tai khoan, ho so thu cung, lich hen, kham benh, grooming, boarding, hoa don, thong bao va danh gia.

## 3. Van de du an giai quyet

Du an giai quyet cac van de thuong gap trong viec tim kiem, quan ly va su dung dich vu cho thu cung:

- Nguoi nuoi thu cung kho theo doi day du thong tin thu cung, lich hen va lich su dich vu.
- Thong tin ve dich vu, lich hen, gia ca hoac trang thai xu ly bi roi rac.
- Doi ngu van hanh can mot noi tap trung de xu ly kham benh, grooming, boarding, hoa don va thong bao.
- Viec phan quyen giua customer, doctor, staff va admin can ro rang.

## 4. Doi tuong nguoi dung

- Customers who manage pet profiles and appointments.
- Doctors who review exams, medical records, schedules, and stats.
- Staff who support appointments, grooming, boarding, reports, and service operations.
- Admins who manage users, settings, services, and system data.

## 5. Gia tri du an mang lai

- Giup nguoi dung tiep can dich vu cho thu cung de dang hon.
- Giam thoi gian tim kiem, lien he va theo doi thong tin dich vu.
- Tao mot noi tap trung de quan ly thong tin, lich hen va trang thai dich vu.
- Ho tro doi ngu noi bo quan ly yeu cau, ho so, lich hen va thanh toan tot hon.

## 6. Chuc nang chinh o muc tong quan

- Quan ly tai khoan, dang ky, dang nhap va phan quyen.
- Quan ly thong tin nguoi dung.
- Quan ly thong tin thu cung.
- Hien thi va quan ly danh sach dich vu cho thu cung.
- Tao va theo doi lich hen/dich vu.
- Quan ly kham benh, don thuoc va tiem phong.
- Quan ly grooming va boarding.
- Quan ly hoa don, thanh toan, thong bao va danh gia.
- Khu vuc quan tri de quan ly du lieu co ban neu can.

## 7. Pham vi MVP

MVP nen tap trung vao phien ban don gian nhat co the dung duoc:

- Trang gioi thieu hoac man hinh chinh cua san pham.
- Dang ky va dang nhap.
- Role-based portals cho customer, doctor, staff va admin.
- Quan ly thong tin co ban cua nguoi dung.
- Quan ly thong tin thu cung co ban.
- Danh sach dich vu co ban.
- Tao lich hen/dang ky dich vu.
- Xem trang thai lich hen/dich vu.
- Tai lieu setup va huong dan manual test toi thieu.

## 8. Nhung gi chua lam trong MVP

- Thanh toan online day du.
- Chat realtime giua nguoi dung va doi ngu dich vu.
- He thong danh gia, binh luan nang cao.
- Goi y dich vu bang AI.
- Ung dung mobile rieng.
- Tich hop ban do, dinh vi hoac giao hang.
- Dashboard thong ke nang cao.
- Tu dong hoa marketing, voucher hoac loyalty program.

## 9. Current stack

- Frontend: React 18, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI components.
- Backend: Express 5, Prisma, Supabase client, JWT, bcrypt.
- Database: PostgreSQL via Prisma schema and migrations.
- Documentation: Mintlify with `.mdx` pages under `/docs`.

## 10. Documentation rules

Every functional change should update the matching feature, API, database, architecture, deployment, or planning page.

## 11. Ghi chu cho nguoi moi vao project

Brief nay la ban nen tang va can duoc cap nhat khi scope san pham thay doi. Neu co quyet dinh moi ve role, MVP, workflow, database hoac deployment, hay cap nhat brief cung voi BRD va master plan.
