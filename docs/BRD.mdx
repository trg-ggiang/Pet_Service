# Business Requirements Document

## Business objective

Pet Service should centralize pet care workflows for appointments, medical care, grooming, boarding, billing, and customer communication.

## Roles

- Guest: unauthenticated visitor.
- Customer: pet owner.
- Doctor: handles medical visits and records.
- Staff: handles operational workflows.
- Admin: manages system data and users.

## Core capabilities

- Registration, login, and role-based access.
- Customer pet profile management.
- Appointment scheduling and status tracking.
- Doctor workflows for exams, records, prescriptions, and vaccinations.
- Staff workflows for grooming, boarding, services, and reports.
- Invoice, notification, payment, and review records.

## Success criteria

- Users can complete core workflows without manual database intervention.
- Backend APIs enforce authentication and role boundaries.
- Database migrations remain reproducible.
- Documentation stays current with implementation.
