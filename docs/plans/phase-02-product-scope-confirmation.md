# Phase 02: Product Scope Confirmation

## 1. Muc tieu phase

Lam ro pham vi san pham MVP, cac role, danh sach dich vu, user flow va nhung gia dinh dang con mo trong BRD.

## 2. Pham vi cong viec

- Ra soat `docs/brief.md` va `docs/BRD.md`.
- Xac nhan mo hinh san pham: marketplace nhieu nha cung cap hay he thong cho mot don vi.
- Xac nhan role trong MVP.
- Xac nhan chuc nang nao bat buoc co trong MVP.
- Ghi lai cac quyet dinh san pham quan trong.

## 3. Checklist chi tiet

- [ ] Doc muc `Can xac nhan them` trong `docs/BRD.md`.
- [ ] Xac nhan mo hinh san pham chinh.
- [ ] Xac nhan danh sach dich vu MVP.
- [ ] Xac nhan Service Provider co tu dang nhap quan ly hay khong.
- [ ] Xac nhan Admin can lam gi trong MVP.
- [ ] Xac nhan co can lich hen theo khung gio hay chi tao yeu cau.
- [ ] Xac nhan ngon ngu, tien te, timezone va dia ban phuc vu.
- [ ] Cap nhat `docs/brief.md` neu co thay doi dinh huong.
- [ ] Cap nhat `docs/BRD.md` neu co quyet dinh moi.

## 4. File/thu muc du kien bi anh huong

- `docs/brief.md`
- `docs/BRD.md`
- `docs/plans/master-plan.md`
- `docs/CHANGELOG.md` neu scope thay doi lon

## 5. Ket qua mong doi

Team co mot pham vi MVP ro rang, biet chinh xac can build gi va chua build gi.

## 6. Cach test manual

- Doc lai `docs/brief.md` va `docs/BRD.md`.
- Kiem tra cac role, flow va MVP co thong nhat voi nhau.
- Kiem tra cac muc chua chac da duoc dua vao `Can xac nhan them` neu chua co cau tra loi.

## 7. Rui ro co the gap

- Scope bi mo rong qua lon cho MVP.
- Role Service Provider va Admin bi trung trach nhiem.
- User flow chua ro nen anh huong database va API.
- Thieu quyet dinh ve lich hen, tien te hoac dia ban.

## 8. Dieu kien Done

- Cac gia dinh quan trong trong BRD da duoc xac nhan hoac ghi ro la chua quyet.
- MVP co danh sach chuc nang ro rang.
- Cac chuc nang sau MVP duoc tach rieng.
- Master plan duoc cap nhat neu scope thay doi.
